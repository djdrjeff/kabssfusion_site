Common questions

Answers before you even ask.

The things customers ask us most, answered straight.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?+
Yes. Delivery is same-day within Nairobi and 24–48 hours countrywide. On-site support is available across Kenya.
What warranty do your laptops come with?+
Refurbished Ex-UK units carry a 6-month warranty and brand-new units a 1-year warranty. Every unit is tested before it leaves us.
Are Ex-UK refurbished laptops reliable?+
Yes — they are genuine HP, Dell and Lenovo business-class machines, fully tested and warrantied. They are excellent value for students and offices.
Who needs to comply with the Kenya Data Protection Act?+
Any business that collects personal data — names, phone numbers, ID numbers or M-Pesa details. Penalties for non-compliance can reach KSh 5 million. We handle the full journey: gap assessment, ODPC registration, policies and ongoing DPO support.
Do you offer ongoing maintenance contracts?+
Yes — annual maintenance contracts (AMC) start from KSh 12,500/month and include quarterly preventive maintenance plus 24/7 emergency response.
How do repairs work?+
Diagnosis is free, and you get a clear quote before any work begins. We handle screens, batteries, keyboards, motherboards, SSD/RAM upgrades and data recovery.
Do you offer cybersecurity services for small businesses in Nairobi?+
Yes — vulnerability assessments and penetration testing (VAPT), managed firewall and endpoint protection, 24/7 monitoring, and phishing/security-awareness training, sized for SMEs, SACCOs, schools and NGOs.
How fast do you respond to server or network emergencies?+
Annual maintenance contract clients get guaranteed SLA response times with 24/7 emergency support. Walk-in and one-off requests are typically actioned the same business day within Nairobi.
Can you build and host a website for my business?+
Yes — from a one-page landing site to a full business or e-commerce website, plus domain registration, SSL, hosting, professional email and an on-page SEO audit so it's found on Google from day one.
Do you also manage social media accounts?+
Yes, as an add-on to our web services — ongoing social media management to keep your business active and consistent online alongside your website.
